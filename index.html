The Beginning of my cheerleading/cheerleader Journey

A little information about me: I started dance when I was in 2nd grade I think, I did gymnastics in 3rd grade, and then in 4th and 5th grade, I did competitive cheer at cheerbratz, you can search it up on like ticktock. I did cheer competitions and ALSTAR cheer. I loved doing ALSTAR cheer, my favorite parts were when I was in the air in stunts and tumbling. I just loved it. I also love making poems. I made one for an assignment in 6th grade in my English class and my teacher loved it!
